headline: Our Implementation
headline_accent: Process
subtext: Every engagement follows our proven 4-step methodology, tailored to your outlet's specific operations and Dubai Municipality requirements.
---

steps:
- number: "01"
  title: Assessment
  body: We visit your premises, evaluate your current setup, collect required documents, and identify gaps against Dubai Municipality standards.

- number: "02"
  title: Configuration
  body: We set up and configure the required systems — DM Check platform, GMP documentation, or hygiene protocols — tailored to your kitchen's workflow.

- number: "03"
  title: Training
  body: Basic training for all food handlers, advanced training for your Person In Charge, and practical hands-on sessions to ensure full team readiness.

- number: "04"
  title: Follow-Up
  body: Post-implementation support with monthly report reviews, compliance monitoring, gap identification, and corrective guidance to keep you fully compliant.
